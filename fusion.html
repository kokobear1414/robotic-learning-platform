Introduction

Robots must perceive and interact with complex environments using sensors that are often noisy or unreliable. Sensor fusion enables robots to combine information from multiple sources to produce accurate and stable estimates.

What Is Sensor Fusion?

Sensor fusion is the process of integrating data from multiple sensors to obtain a better understanding of a system than any individual sensor can provide on its own.

How Sensor Fusion Works

  • Prediction: Estimates from motion or physics models
  • Measurement: Real sensor data affected by noise
In this module, a Kalman Filter is used to optimally balance prediction and measurement based on uncertainty.
